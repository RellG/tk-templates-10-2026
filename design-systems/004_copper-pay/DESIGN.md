# Copper Pay

> Every payment, perfectly in place.

**Theme:** dark

## Origin
Original TKTechnology design direction, October 2026. Fictional demo brand. No third-party site was scraped for this collection.

## Layout
Two-column split hero with text-left. Include stats pricing faq. Use 2px card corners, generous whitespace, and a responsive mobile stack.
