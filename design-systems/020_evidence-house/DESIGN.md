# Evidence House

> Turn your controls into clear evidence.

**Theme:** light

## Origin
Original TKTechnology design direction, October 2026. Fictional demo brand. No third-party site was scraped for this collection.

## Layout
Editorial asymmetric spread with column rules. Include tabs quotes faq. Use 2px card corners, generous whitespace, and a responsive mobile stack.
